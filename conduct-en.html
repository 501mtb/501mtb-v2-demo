501MTB — Internal Code of Conduct

1. MTB rides

  • Respect for time: arrive at the agreed meeting place and time.
  • Safety first: safety is a collective responsibility of the whole team. Every member contributes to group safety, looks out for others and helps keep the ride safe for everyone.
  • Team spirit: no participant should be deliberately left behind. The group should adapt, where possible, to different skill and fitness levels.
  • Respect the route: follow the ride leader’s instructions, respect other road users, private property and the environment.
  • Communication: report mechanical problems, injuries, serious fatigue or any need to leave the group.
  • Respectful behaviour: no provocation, humiliation, discrimination or deliberately dangerous conduct.
  • Know your limits: each rider should assess their ability and speak up if they cannot keep up.
  • Ride leader: organizes the ride, gives safety instructions and may change the route or stop the ride if conditions become dangerous.

2. 501MTB Messenger chat

  • The official space for organizing activities and strengthening community ties.
  • Be respectful and polite, including during disagreements.
  • Keep messages relevant to MTB, rides, events, training and community life.
  • No public arguments, insults, threats or provocation.
  • No spam, repeated posts, unauthorized advertising or unrelated content.
  • Do not share private conversations, contact details or photos without consent.
  • Official information must be confirmed by the appropriate responsible person.
  • Admins may pin rules, ride details and announcements. Respect moderation; raise disagreements calmly in private.

3. General conduct

  • This code applies on rides, online and at events.
  • Protect the reputation of 501MTB.
  • Show solidarity: help other riders, share knowledge and encourage beginners.
  • Be honest: do not knowingly publish false information on behalf of 501MTB or claim to represent the organization without authorization.
  • Respect organizational decisions; constructive feedback is welcome.
  • Take care of shared equipment and leave ride locations clean.
  • No threats, aggression, intimidation or discrimination.
  • Voluntary participation depends on each person’s availability and capacity.

4. Application and sanctions

Measures should be proportionate to the seriousness and repetition of the breach:

  1. Friendly reminder.
  2. Official warning.
  3. Temporary suspension from activities or the Messenger group.
  4. Exclusion for serious or repeated violations.

Where possible, the member should be given an opportunity to explain their side before a major decision. Administrators should act fairly and avoid arbitrary sanctions.
